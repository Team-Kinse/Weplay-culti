Event Rules

How the table works
• Every round starts with a 15-second countdown.
• Betting is open only while the timer is above 0.
• Tapping the same lucky zone again adds another stack to that zone. Every lucky zone stays available permanently; its ROUNDS counter shows how many completed rounds have passed since that zone last hit.
• At 0, all bets lock. One shared 5-card table is completed and each side reveals 2 kicker cards. The first table card was already visible during betting.
• Each side builds the strongest 5-card poker pattern from the 5 shared table cards + its own 2 kickers. The stronger poker hand wins: Royal Flush > Straight Flush > Four of a Kind > Full House > Flush > Straight > Three of a Kind > Two Pairs > One Pair > High Card.
• Equal hands are a tie after normal kicker comparison.
• Lucky Zone: the winning side's kicker cards can hit Suited / Connector / Suited Connector, plus kicker Pair and AA. Pair / Any Pair only hits when the winner's kicker cards themselves are a pair. The High Card / One Pair, Two Pairs, Three of a Kind / Straight / Flush, Full House, and Four of a Kind / Straight Flush / Royal Flush zones only hit from the winning side's completed poker pattern.
• Kicker-based and completed-pattern zones can both hit in the same round.
• Every round is generated from a freshly shuffled deck using browser RNG.
Fund safety: the starting fund is 1,000. If your fund is below the minimum bet of 50 at the start of a betting phase, it is automatically replenished to 1,000.
